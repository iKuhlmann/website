About

My main research area is symbolic artificial intelligence, with a focus on knowledge representation and reasoning (KR). I am particularly interested in how to reason with uncertain or inconsistent information and how inconsistency can be quantified and handled computationally. Another strand of my research concerns computational argumentation, including approaches that combine (KR) and machine learning.
