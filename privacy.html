2. Hosting

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub.

The processing is carried out for the purpose of providing this website securely and reliably. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest lies in the secure and reliable provision of this website.

GitHub may process personal data in the United States and other countries outside the European Economic Area. According to GitHub, international data transfers are protected, where applicable, by mechanisms including the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. GitHub also participates in the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

Further information about the processing of personal data by GitHub can be found in GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

This website contains links to external websites and services. No data is transmitted to these services merely because such a link is displayed on this website. If you follow an external link, the privacy policy of the respective provider applies.

5. Contact by email

If you contact me by email, I process the information contained in your message, including your email address and any personal data you provide, for the purpose of responding to your enquiry.

The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. My legitimate interest lies in responding to enquiries addressed to me. Where the communication is related to the initiation or performance of a contractual relationship, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR may additionally apply.

I retain correspondence only for as long as necessary for the respective purpose, unless statutory retention obligations require longer storage.

6. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you may have the right to:

  • request deletion of your personal data;
  • request restriction of processing;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests; and
  • receive personal data in a portable format where the applicable legal requirements are met.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection supervisory authority.

To exercise your rights with respect to processing for which I am responsible, you may contact me using the email address provided above.

I may update this Privacy Policy if the website or the applicable legal requirements change.
